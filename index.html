Questions attorneys ask

How do I calculate a federal court deadline under FRCP Rule 6(a)?

Exclude the day of the triggering event, count every day including weekends and holidays, and include the last day. If the last day is a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday, the period runs until the next day that is not.

Does the calculator add 3 days for service by mail?

Yes. Tick the mail service box and it adds 3 days under Rule 6(d), then applies the weekend and holiday rule again to the new date.

What does billing in 0.1-hour increments mean?

Time is rounded up to the next 6 minutes, so 7 minutes bills as 0.2 hours. The tracker also supports 0.25-hour (15-minute) rounding.

Is my time or client data stored on a server?

No. Time entries stay in your own browser storage on your device, and nothing is sent to a server.
