Court deadline calculator

Format YYYY-MM-DD, separated by commas.

Enter a triggering date and the number of days. The result shows the final deadline and any weekends or holidays that moved it.

Follows FRCP 6(a): the trigger day is excluded, every day is counted, and a deadline landing on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday moves to the next day that is not (earlier, when counting backward). Local rules, court orders and state rules can differ, so confirm every deadline against the governing rule.
